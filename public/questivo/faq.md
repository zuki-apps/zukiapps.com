## What is Questivo?

Questivo is a mobile app for scavenger hunts, quiz trails, and real-world missions. Hosts build quests and share a game code. Players join instantly — no account required to play. Package: com.zuki.apps.questivo.

## Do players need an account?

No account is required to play. Players join with a nickname and a game code. Hosts can sign in with Google to save and sync quests.

## How do I join a quest?

Open Questivo, tap Play, enter the host's game code, or scan the quest QR code. No registration needed to play.

## What permissions does Questivo use?

Camera for photo missions, photos for image selection, and location for GPS checkpoint missions only. Deny permissions and the related mission types will not work. Indoor quests do not use GPS.

## Is Questivo free?

Free to download. Many quests are free to play. Some quests use a one-time unlock. Host Pro is a subscription for larger live rooms — 5 guests free, 50 with Pro. There are no ads.

## Can I create my own quest?

Yes. Use the guided wizard to add riddles, photo missions, GPS checkpoints, QR scans, and a story. Share a code or QR with players.

## What mission types does Questivo support?

Riddles, photo capture, GPS checkpoint arrivals, QR code scans, and numeric/text codes. Indoor, outdoor, or hybrid quest layouts are supported.

## Does Questivo track my location continuously?

Only when a quest has a GPS checkpoint and you allow location. The phone checks that you arrived at the checkpoint. Location is not tracked continuously.

## Can I delete my host account?

Yes. In the app: Settings → Delete account. Or use the form at https://zukiapps.com/questivo/delete-account.
