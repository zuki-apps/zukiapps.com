# FAQ — DreamBit Apps legacy archive

Canonical page: https://zukiapps.com/dreambit-legacy

## Are DreamBit Apps still updated?

No. Titles on this archive page are historic and unmaintained. They may fail on modern OS versions. Prefer current Zuki Apps products when available.

## Is Zuki Apps the same developer as DreamBit Apps?

Yes. DreamBit Apps was the earlier publishing name. Zuki Apps hosts this archive and publishes the current product lineup on zukiapps.com.

## Where can I find the UpdateStar Excellent award?

Whistle Camera - Legacy earned UpdateStar’s Excellent award from verified installers (5/5). That applies to the legacy UpdateStar product page, not App Store or Google Play star ratings for the current Whistle Camera.

## Do the Google Play links still work?

Where a listing remains on Google Play, the button opens that package ID. Some older listings may disappear from the store over time; this page still documents the historic package names.
