## What is Roam Guard?

Roam Guard is an Android app for travelers that monitors per-app mobile data in real time and warns you before your eSIM or roaming package runs out. Package: com.zuki.apps.roamguard.

## Does Roam Guard upload my usage to the cloud?

No. Usage statistics are processed and stored on your device. You may manually export a CSV (Pro feature) and share it yourself. Optional crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics are sent only if the app crashes.

## Does Roam Guard read my browsing history?

No. Roam Guard uses Android usage and network stats, not browser history.

## What is the VPN for?

Ultra Saver mode uses a local VPN on your phone to block mobile data for apps you have not approved. Traffic is not sent to Roam Guard servers — it stays on-device.

## Is Roam Guard free?

Yes. Core monitoring, alerts, travel modes, and one travel profile are free. Roam Guard Pro is an optional one-time upgrade via Google Play — no subscription.

## Does Roam Guard work on iPhone?

Roam Guard v1.0.0 is Android only (Android 10 / API 29 or later).

## Why does Roam Guard need Usage Access permission?

Android requires this special permission for apps to read per-app mobile data usage. Without it, Roam Guard cannot monitor which apps are using data.

## Can I monitor two SIMs or an eSIM separately?

Yes, on dual-SIM Android phones. Choose which line to monitor in the app. Dashboard, alerts, and statistics apply to the selected line only. Per-line stats may require Phone permission. Some device manufacturers still report combined mobile data regardless of permission — Roam Guard explains this in the app when it applies.

## What are smart detections?

Heuristics that label likely usage patterns — photo backup, video streaming, cloud sync, app updates, and large downloads — so you know what is draining your package.

## What does Roam Guard Pro include?

Unlimited travel profiles, unlimited app ignore rules, full usage history, CSV export, home screen widget, Quick Settings tile, premium themes, and full smart insights. One-time purchase — no subscription.

## How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Same Google account → Settings → Upgrade to Pro → Restore purchases.
