## What is DrawingPilot?

An AR drawing and tracing app. A reference image floats over the live camera so it can be traced onto paper. Package: com.zuki.apps.drawingpilot.

## Is DrawingPilot free?

Yes. It is free to download and use, and it shows ads (Google AdMob).

## Do you upload the camera or my drawings?

No. The camera is a live guide. Photos, projects and on-device line art stay on the device unless you save, share or export them.

## How does Pinterest import work?

If you connect Pinterest, the app signs in anonymously and our server stores the OAuth tokens so it can read your boards and pins. We do not post to Pinterest. The connection is off until the server is deployed.

## Can I change ad consent?

Yes. Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone, the App Tracking Transparency prompt asks before the advertising identifier is used.
