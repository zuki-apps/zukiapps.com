## What is DrawingPilot?

An AR drawing and tracing app. A reference image floats over the live camera so it can be traced onto paper. No account is needed. Package: com.zuki.apps.drawingpilot.

## Is DrawingPilot free?

Yes. It is free to download and use, with ads (Google AdMob) and a few limits: 10 projects, 60-second recordings, line art and pencil styles. DrawingPilot Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that removes ads and unlocks unlimited projects, HD conversions, blueprint and cartoon styles and recordings of any length. It is not a subscription.

## Where can images come from?

Your gallery, the camera, Files or Google Drive, the clipboard, Share → DrawingPilot from any app (Photos, Safari, Pinterest, WhatsApp…), or free images from Openverse.

## Where do the free images come from?

Openverse, a search engine for Creative Commons and public-domain images run by the WordPress Foundation. Searches go directly from the app to Openverse. The creator and licence are shown before you use an image.

## Do you upload the camera or my drawings?

No. The camera is a live guide. Photos, projects, line art and videos stay on the device unless you save, share or export them. DrawingPilot has no server of its own.

## Can I export a time-lapse for Reels or TikTok?

Yes. Record a tracing session, then Export: Reels/TikTok (9:16), square (1:1) or original, at up to 16× speed. The video is saved to your photo library and shared.

## Can I change ad consent?

Yes. Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone, the App Tracking Transparency prompt asks before the advertising identifier is used.
