## What is Tricky Rooms?

A cosy puzzle game with 30 hand-drawn rooms. Each one hides exactly one thing that's wrong — find it in a few taps, fix the room, and open the next door. 30 rooms in 6 themed halls. Package: com.zuki.apps.trickyrooms.

## Is Tricky Rooms free?

Yes. It's free to download and play, with optional in-app purchases: refill lives, buy a pack of 5 hints, or unlock Unlimited (unlimited lives and no banner or full-screen ads).

## What age is it for?

Everyone. Cartoon art, no violence, no chat and no user-generated content.

## Does it work offline?

Yes. All 30 rooms play offline. Ads, purchases and sharing need a connection.

## Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is saved on your device.

## How many rooms are there?

30 rooms in 6 halls of 5 doors. Doors open in order.

## How many taps do I get?

5 per room in the first hall, then 4, 3, and 2 in the last hall. A miss leaves a red scratch and costs one.

## How do lives work?

You have 5. Failing a room, trying again, or leaving a room after a tap costs one; backing out before your first tap is free. A new life arrives every 30 minutes, even with the app closed.

## I'm stuck — any help?

Share the room picture with friends from the fail screen, or watch a short video to see where to look. Out of taps? A video gives you two more.

## Is this the old Tricky Rooms?

It's a remaster of the 2014 original: the same rooms, art and music, rebuilt for today's phones and tablets. Progress from the old app doesn't carry over.

## Will there be more rooms?

Yes — more halls are planned and will arrive in free updates. Your progress carries straight on into them.

## What ads are there?

A banner on the win and fail screens, occasional full-screen ads between rooms (never in your first session or first five rooms), and optional videos you choose to watch for a life or a hint.

## How do I restore Unlimited?

Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

## How do I contact you?

In the app: Settings → Send feedback. Or email zuki.apps.dev@gmail.com.
