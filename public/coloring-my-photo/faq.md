## What is Coloring My Photo?

Coloring My Photo is an app that turns your personal photos into interactive color-by-number pages. All processing happens on your device. Package: com.zuki.apps.coloringmyphoto.

## Does my photo leave the phone?

No. Photos are processed locally to create coloring pages. Zuki Apps does not upload your photos to any server.

## How many colors can I use?

12, 16, 24, or 32 colors. Free goes up to 16 (plus one Premium-quality trial). Premium unlocks 32 colors.

## What is Easy / Medium / Advanced?

How detailed the coloring page is. Easy produces fewer, larger regions. Advanced is a Premium unlock.

## Do I need an account?

No account is required. Preferences and saved projects are stored on your device.

## Can I drop the background?

Yes — subject isolation keeps the person, pet, or object and turns the background into blank paper.

## What does Premium include?

Premium removes ads and the export watermark, unlocks Advanced detail, 32 colors, PDF export, and the subject isolation feature.

## What photos work best?

Photos where the subject fills the frame with even lighting. Pets, portraits, and single objects work well. Busy crowd photos or very dark/flash-split images may produce noisy regions.

## What if processing fails?

Try a clearer photo with good lighting, use Easy detail level, and make sure the image is not extremely small or busy.

## Can I restore Premium?

Yes. Use Restore on the Premium screen with the same account you used to purchase.

## How do I share a coloring page?

Save as an image, share a .cmpage file to continue on another device, or export as a PDF with Premium.
