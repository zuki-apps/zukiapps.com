## What is TallyNudge?

TallyNudge is a dark, tactile tally counter and clicker for iOS by Zuki Apps. Free on the App Store (id6814104401). Google Play coming soon. Package: `com.zuki.apps.tallynudge`.

## Do I need an account?

No. Counters stay on your device. Crash and usage diagnostics go to Firebase.

## What is included for free?

Up to 4 counters, core counting, and optional ads (AdMob banner plus occasional interstitial, 4-minute cooldown). Premium unlocks unlimited counters, history, volume-button counting, extra widget layouts, and no ads.

## Does TallyNudge have Home Screen widgets?

Not in 0.9.0. The Widgets tab shows four in-app preview layouts so you can pick how a counter looks. Those are not iOS or Android Home Screen widgets.

## What is the Premium product ID?

`tallynudge_premium`. Recommended price USD $2.99, one-time, not a subscription. Payments are processed by Apple. Google Play coming soon.

## Does TallyNudge show ads?

Yes on the free plan: Google AdMob banner and interstitial with a 4-minute cooldown. Premium removes ads.

## Can I count with volume buttons?

Yes on Premium. Volume up increments, volume down decrements, so you can keep your eyes on the set.

## Where are my counters stored?

On your device. Uninstalling the app deletes local counters and history.

## How do I restore Premium?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases with the same Apple ID. Product ID: `tallynudge_premium`. Google Play coming soon.

## How do I get support?

Email zuki.apps.dev@gmail.com or visit https://zukiapps.com/tally-nudge/support.
