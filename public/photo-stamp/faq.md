## What is Photo Stamp?

Photo Stamp adds date, time, and location to photos and videos you already have. Processing stays entirely on your device. Package: com.zuki.apps.photostamp.

## Are my photos uploaded?

No. There is no account and no upload. All media stays on your device. No AI cloud processing.

## Does Photo Stamp overwrite originals?

No. Export creates a new file. The original photo or video is never overwritten.

## Do you need location permission?

No GPS permission is required to stamp your gallery. Location appears on the stamp only if the file already has it in its EXIF metadata, or if you type a place name manually.

## Is this a GPS camera or AI editor?

No. Photo Stamp reads existing EXIF metadata from files already in your library. It is not a GPS camera, survey tool, or AI editor.

## What is free vs Photo Stamp Pro?

Free includes 30 photo and 3 video exports per week, plus ads (never in the editor or during export). Photo Stamp Pro is a $4.99 one-time unlock (com.zuki.apps.photostamp.premium_lifetime) for unlimited exports, premium styles, full resolution, and no ads.

## How long does stamping take?

Most stamps take about 10–20 seconds. Everything processes on your device.

## Does Photo Stamp work with videos?

Yes. Select a video from your library and stamp it the same way as a photo.

## How do I restore Pro after reinstalling?
