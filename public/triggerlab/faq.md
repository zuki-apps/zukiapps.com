## What is TriggerLab?

An event timer: it measures the time between a start event and a stop event, down to the millisecond. A large clock readable from across the room, laps, multi-stage runs and a reaction test. Package: com.zuki.apps.triggerlab.

## Is TriggerLab free?

Yes. It's free to download and use, and it shows ads (Google AdMob).

## How precise is it?

Times come from the device's monotonic clock and are stored in microseconds. Display precision (seconds, tenths, hundredths, milliseconds) only changes formatting, never the stored value.

## Does it keep timing while the phone is locked?

Yes. TriggerLab adds the time the device spent asleep, so a run across a locked screen stays correct.

## Which measurement modes are there?

Single, repeated, laps, multi-stage and reaction. Start and stop with the on-screen buttons or the volume keys. Camera, microphone and location triggers are not in this version.

## Do I need an account? Where is my data?

No account. Measurements, history and profiles stay on your device. Export results as CSV through the share sheet.
