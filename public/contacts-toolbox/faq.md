## What is Contacts Toolbox?

Contacts Toolbox is a contacts organizer for Android. It finds duplicates, enables bulk edits, and shows insights about incomplete contacts — entirely on your device. Free on Google Play. Package: com.zuki.apps.contactstoolbox. App Store coming soon.

## Does Contacts Toolbox read my phone contacts?

Yes. After you grant permission, it reads and updates the system Contacts app on this device. Matching and edits stay on device — nothing is uploaded.

## Does it upload my contacts?

No. Matching, merges, and bulk edits run entirely on your device. No account or upload is required.

## Do I need an account?

No. Contacts Toolbox works without any sign-in or account.

## What is free vs Premium?

Free includes browse, scan, and duplicate preview. Premium (product ID: contacts_toolbox_premium) unlocks applying merges, bulk edits, insights, CSV export, and removes ads.

## Can I undo a merge or bulk edit?

Yes. History snapshots let you reverse operations you have applied.

## What formats can I export?

CSV export is a Premium feature. Free users can preview suggestions without exporting.
