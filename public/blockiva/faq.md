## What is Blockiva – Block Puzzle?

Blockiva is a rotating-rules block puzzle for iOS and Android by Zuki Apps. Place shapes, clear rows and columns, then survive the next rule. Tagline: Place. Clear. Survive the rule. Package: `com.zuki.apps.blockiva`. Store listings coming soon.

## Is this Block Blast?

No. Blockiva is its own rotating-rules puzzle. We do not name competitors in ads.

## Do I need an account?

No. Scores and settings stay on your device.

## What are the five rotating rules?

Locked Tiles, Frozen Row, Shadow Blocks, Double Score, and Fake Block. A new rule appears every N turns.

## What difficulties are available?

Chill 10×10 (5 turns), Classic 8×8 (3 turns), Intense 7×7 (2 turns), and Chaos 6×6 (1 turn).

## How do ads work?

Free players may see a menu banner and an interstitial after game over, with a 2-minute cooldown. Remove Ads (`blockiva_remove_ads`) is a one-time purchase. Recommended price USD $2.99.

## How many shuffles do I get?

One shuffle per game on Free. When nothing fits, the run ends. Premium adds extra shuffles and a continue.

## Does Fake Block count toward clears?

No. Fake Block does not count toward clears — do not try to complete a line with it.

## Is Remove Ads a subscription?

No. `blockiva_remove_ads` is a one-time in-app purchase.
